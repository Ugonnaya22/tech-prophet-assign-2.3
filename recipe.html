How to Prepare Nigerian Jollof Rice

Jollof rice is a classic West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, onions and a rich blend of spices. This recipe is for a simple, flavorful pot of party-style Jollof.

Safety and Cooking Notes

Warning: Scotch bonnet peppers are extremely hot. Do not touch your eyes while handling them. Always wash your hands thoroughly with soap immediately after chopping the peppers.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of long-grain parboiled rice
  • 4 large tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers (tatashe)
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper (ata rodo)
  • 2 medium onions
  • 1/2 cup of cooking oil
  • 2 tablespoons of tomato paste
  • Chicken stock or water
  • Salt, thyme and curry powder to taste

Preparation Steps

  1. Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, scotch bonnet, and one onion until smooth.
  2. Heat the oil in a large pot and fry the remaining chopped onion until translucent.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste and fry for about 3 minutes to remove the sour taste.
  4. Pour in the blended pepper mix and let it cook down until the water reduces and the oil separates.
  5. Add your chicken stock, salt, thyme and curry powder. Bring the mixture to a boil.
  6. Wash the rice thoroughly to remove excess starch, then stir it into the boiling sauce.
  7. Cover the pot tightly with foil to trap the steam, lower the heat and cook until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed.

Jollof Cooking Terminology

Party Jollof
A specific style of Jollof rice cooked over firewood, giving it a distinct, slightly smoky flavor that is popular at weddings and events.
Parboil
To partially boil the rice beforehand to remove excess surface starch, preventing the final dish from becoming soggy or mushy.
Obe Ata
The rich, blended pepper and tomato base that forms the foundation of the Jollof stew.
